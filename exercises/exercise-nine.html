Title Recorded Meteorites Around the World

An interactive spatial visualisation of meteorite records by geographic position, time period and mass.

Summary

This interactive spatial visualisation builds upon my Module 8 meteorite map, using NASA's Meteorite Landings dataset. Each marker represents an individual meteorite record, positioned using its latitude and longitude. Colour represents the recorded time period, while marker size represents mass.

Mapping
  • Geographic Position: Latitude and longitude of each recorded meteorite.
  • Colour: Six recorded time periods: Before 1800, 1800–1849, 1850–1899, 1900–1949, 1950–1999 and 2000 Onwards.
  • Marker Size: Recorded meteorite mass, represented using a logarithmic scale.

Important Notes
  • The map represents meteorite records in the dataset, not every meteorite that has landed on Earth.
  • Records with missing latitude, longitude, year or mass were excluded.
  • Records located at (0°, 0°) were excluded.
  • The original mass data are measured in grams, although some legend values are expressed in kilograms.
  • Marker sizes use a logarithmic scale rather than being directly proportional to raw mass.
  • Geographic clusters reflect recorded observations and should not be interpreted as the actual likelihood of meteorite landings.
